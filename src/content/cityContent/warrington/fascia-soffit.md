## Frequently asked questions
**How do I know if my fascia or soffit needs replacing?**
Look for flaking paint, soft or spongy timber, gaps where the board has pulled away from the wall, or birds/wasps getting into the roof space through a broken soffit. Any of these are worth a free inspection.

**Is uPVC better than timber?**
For most homes, yes - uPVC fascia and soffit boards don't rot, don't need repainting, and typically come with a 10+ year guarantee, whereas timber needs repainting every few years to avoid rot returning.
