## The chimney repairs we're asked for most in Halifax

### Repointing stone chimney stacks
Gritstone stacks were built with soft lime mortar, and that is what they should be repointed with. Hard cement pointing looks tidy for a few years, then traps water behind it and makes the stone face flake away. A good installer will rake out the failed joints and repoint in a lime mix that lets the stack breathe. Our [chimney repointing cost guide](/blog/chimney-repointing-cost-signs-england/) covers the warning signs and costs in more detail.

### Lead flashing repair
On exposed Halifax roofs, a split or lifted flashing lets water run straight down the chimney breast. The lasting fix is new lead, properly dressed and wedged into the joints, rather than another layer of sealant.

### Flaunching, pots and cowls
Cracked flaunching (the mortar cap holding the pots) and loose pots are common after a stormy winter on the valley sides. Re-flaunching usually goes hand in hand with re-bedding or replacing pots, and fitting cowls or caps to flues that aren't used any more.

### Chimney rebuild and restoration
Where a stack is leaning or the stone has badly weathered, rebuilding from sound masonry may be the only safe option. Installers can reuse the original stone or source matching reclaimed stone, which matters on Halifax terraces where every stack in the row looks the same.

## Frequently asked questions
**Should a stone chimney in Halifax be repointed with lime or cement?**
Lime, in almost every case. Gritstone stacks were built with lime mortar, and cement repointing traps moisture and damages the stone over time.

**Why is my chimney breast damp after heavy rain?**
On exposed Calderdale roofs, the usual culprits are failed lead flashing, cracked flaunching or porous pointing letting wind-driven rain into the stack. An installer can tell which from a close inspection.

**Who pays to repair a chimney shared with my neighbour?**
A stack that straddles two terraced houses is usually shared, so the cost of repairs is normally split between the owners. Check your deeds and agree the work before it starts.

**Do I need permission to repair a chimney in a conservation area?**
Like-for-like repairs usually don't need permission, but removing a stack or changing its materials in a conservation area, or on a listed building, can. Check with Calderdale Council's planning team first.
