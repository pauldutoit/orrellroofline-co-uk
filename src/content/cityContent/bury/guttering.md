## Frequently asked questions
**How often should gutters be cleared?**
Once or twice a year is typical for most homes - more often if you're near overhanging trees. A blocked gutter that overflows for a full winter can already start to damage fascias and brickwork.

**Can you repair cast-iron guttering instead of replacing it?**
Often, yes - on period properties we'll re-seal joints and treat rust where the iron itself is sound, which is usually cheaper than a full replacement and keeps the original character of the building.
